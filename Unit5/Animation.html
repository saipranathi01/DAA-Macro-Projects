How to read the tree

For a partial tour, LB = cost so far + MST of unvisited cities + cheapest edge from the current city to an unvisited city + cheapest edge from an unvisited city back to A. When one city remains, its MST cost is zero.

Once a complete tour costs 80, branches with LB > 80 are pruned. Equal bounds are kept so the animation can find both optimal tours.
